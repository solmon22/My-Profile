Why I Chose QA

I decided to make a career change after feeling a strong desire to return to a more technological environment.
For the past five years, I worked in public housing, where I realized that the part I enjoyed most was optimizing existing systems for daily operations. I found bugs and collaborated with developers to add features—such as converting PDF reports into live Excel sheets—so I could extract and analyze data much more efficiently.
From there, taking the next step toward my future felt natural. In the past, I studied Software Practical Engineering and later web design and development. I have worked in a variety of roles and gained diverse skills from customer service, in-person training, technical support, as well as designing and building WordPress websites.
Programming languages and collaborating with developers are familiar territory for me. Above all, my defining trait is a genuine passion for fixing, refining, and improving software systems.
