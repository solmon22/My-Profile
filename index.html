Why I Chose QA

I decided to make a career change after feeling a strong desire to return to a more technological environment.
For the past five years, I have worked in public housing, where I discovered that one of the aspects I enjoyed most was finding ways to improve and optimize existing systems and processes. I identified bugs, worked closely with developers, and initiated practical solutions to make everyday tasks more efficient.
For example, I helped transform PDF reports into dynamic Excel files, allowing me to extract, organize, and analyze data much more effectively.
This experience made me realize that I naturally enjoy investigating how systems work, identifying problems, and finding better solutions. Taking the next step toward a career in QA therefore felt like a natural progression for me.
Earlier in my career, I studied Software Engineering, followed by web design and development. I have also gained diverse experience in customer service, technical support, training, and designing and building WordPress websites. I’m comfortable working with technology, communicating with developers, and learning new systems.
Most importantly, I have a genuine passion for finding problems, improving processes, and making software work better — and I’m excited to bring that mindset into the world of QA.
